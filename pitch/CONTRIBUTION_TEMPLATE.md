## 填写说明 / Instructions

- 只记录实际完成的工作，涵盖本次 Pitch 及此前准备。每项任务单列一行，明确自己的贡献及估计耗时（小时）；可增删行。  
  Record only actual completed work for the pitch and its preparation. Use one row per task and state your own contribution and estimated hours; add or remove rows as needed.
- 可回顾会议、研究、数据准备或分析、PPT 设计、讲稿撰写、审核、排练和演讲等方面。这些仅为填写提示，不表示已完成。  
  Consider meetings, research, data preparation or analysis, slide design, script writing, review, rehearsal, and presentation delivery. These are prompts, not claims of completed work.
- 不重复计算同一工作的时间；各成员无需填相同工时。课堂展示尚未发生时，不把未来演讲记作已完成贡献。  
  Do not double-count time. Members do not need equal hours. Do not record future presentation delivery as completed work.
- 每位成员核对自己的记录，删除占位符、未使用行和模板提示后再提交；勿直接提交空白模板。  
  Each member should verify their entries. Remove placeholders, unused rows, and template instructions before submitting; do not submit the blank template.
