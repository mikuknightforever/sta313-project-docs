## 项目目标 / Project Goal

最终成果为一个以数据分析和可视化展示为核心、支持数据探索的交互式网站。先明确受众、研究范围和研究问题，再推进数据准备、分析、图表设计及网站开发。

The final product will be an interactive website centered on data analysis and visualization, supporting data exploration. First define the audience, scope, and research questions; then proceed with data preparation, analysis, chart design, and website development.
