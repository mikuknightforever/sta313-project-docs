选题应兼顾数据可用性与研究价值。较大的问题可通过限定人群、地区、时间或社会事件缩小范围。现有数据不足以支持直接的因果推断，表述应侧重差异、趋势和结构变化。使用外部数据时需注明来源。

Question selection should consider both data availability and research value. Broad questions can be narrowed by population, geography, time, or a social event. The available data do not by themselves support direct causal conclusions; questions should focus on differences, trends, and structural changes. Cite the sources of any external data.
