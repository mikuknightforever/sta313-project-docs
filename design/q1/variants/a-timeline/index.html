Read this first: what the numbers measure

A case is not a person

A case (benefit unit) can include a spouse and children. Every count on this page is cases, not people, and is not adjusted for population.

Two programs, two sizes

Ontario Works (OW) and the Ontario Disability Support Program (ODSP) are counted separately. Switch to the index (2019 Q4 = 100) to compare their relative change on one scale.

Pandemic period

March 2020 – May 2023 (dataset definition). In these quarterly data that is 2020 Q1 – 2023 Q1. Quarters are labelled by their last month.

Context, not cause

The unemployment rate (Ontario, monthly, Jan 2017 – Dec 2025) is shown for context only. Province-wide OW counts include decimals in the source and are shown as published.
