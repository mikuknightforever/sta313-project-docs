Read this first: what the numbers measure

Two different series

The long series (Figure 1) counts all social assistance cases: since April 1998 OW, ODSP, Temporary Care Assistance and Assistance for Children with Severe Disabilities; before 1998 the programs that preceded them. It is never added to or joined with OW and ODSP.

A case is not a person

A case can include a spouse and children. Counts are not adjusted for population, so a record number of cases is not a record share of the population.

Pandemic period

March 2020 – May 2023 (2020 Q1 – 2023 Q1 in the quarterly data).

Shown as published

Some provincial counts include decimals in the source; they are shown as published, rounded to whole cases. The unemployment rate (Jan 2017 – Dec 2025) is context only.
