Did OW and ODSP grow together? Follow one path from 2003 to 2026

Loading data…

Motivation. The question is about how two series moved relative to each other. Plotting one against the other turns "together or apart" into the direction of a single path.

Appeal. A long rightward drift before 2020, then a sharp drop straight down and back up: a shape that is easy to remember and to retell in a briefing.

Cost. Unfamiliar; time runs along the path, not an axis, so readers confuse order and direction more often (Haroz et al., 2016). It needs the reading key and the companion timeline.

Read this first: how to read the path

One dot per quarter

Across = ODSP cases, up = OW cases. Dots are joined in time order, from 2003 Q1 to the latest quarter. The slider and the timeline below show where you are in time.

Direction means something

Moving right = ODSP grew; moving up = OW grew. Both axes use the same scale per index point (2019 Q4 = 100), so the dotted 45° line marks equal relative growth.

A case is not a person

A case can include a spouse and children. Counts are not adjusted for population. Province-wide OW counts include decimals in the source and are shown as published.

Pandemic period and context

March 2020 – May 2023 (2020 Q1 – 2023 Q1). Segments are shaded light → dark: before, during, after. Unemployment (Jan 2017 – Dec 2025) is context only.
