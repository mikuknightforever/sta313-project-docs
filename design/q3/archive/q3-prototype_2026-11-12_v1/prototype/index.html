Read this first: what the numbers measure

Duration = time on assistance so far

Each quarter counts the OW cases open at that moment, grouped by how long each case has received assistance continuously so far. It is a snapshot of the current caseload, not how long a new case will last, and not the chance that a person stays on assistance.

Long-term = 19 months or more

Long-term share = cases open 19+ months ÷ all OW cases in the same area and quarter. The 0–4 month group is not the same as new applications.

A case is not a person

A case (benefit unit) can include a spouse and children. All counts on this page are cases.

CMA ≠ Ontario

Regional figures cover 15 census metropolitan areas (CMAs). They do not add up to the Ontario total, which also includes areas outside CMAs. Ontario values are shown as published.
